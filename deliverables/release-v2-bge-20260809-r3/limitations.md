# Known MVP Limitations

- The 100-request single-instance sample is MVP evidence and is not a production capacity guarantee.
- The local BGE cost uses a declared USD 0.50 per GPU-hour workstation allocation rate and the measured load duration.
- Historical comparison reports are partial cross-profile supporting evidence and are not used for mandatory quality, performance, or cost gates.
- Evaluation-only judge cost is unavailable and is not represented as zero.
